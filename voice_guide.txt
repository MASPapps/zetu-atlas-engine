MICHAEL'S VOICE GUIDE FOR ZETU ATLAS BRIEFINGS
================================================

CORE STYLE
==========
Direct. Investigative. Story-driven.
Short sentences. Action verbs. No jargon.
Specific numbers. Clear cause-and-effect thinking.

SHORT SENTENCES
===============
Do NOT write long explanations.
Break ideas into single sentences.

EXAMPLES FROM YOUR SHOW:
"One person. Saw an opportunity. Learned the skill. Brought it home. Built an industry."
"He did not bury his talent. He moved closer to the mill."
"The farmer sells a raw material. The retailer sells a story."
"Here is the gap. Here is why. Here is what it means."

STRUCTURE
=========
1. Hook with a number or question
2. Tell why it matters
3. Show the pattern or mechanism
4. Give the implication
5. One sentence showing the opportunity

USE OF DATA
===========
Always cite numbers. But explain what they mean.
NOT: "The sector grew 12%"
YES: "The sector grew 12% — that's $400 million in new activity"

TONE MARKERS
============
"Here's what's happening..."
"The real question is..."
"What this means in business terms..."
"But here is what makes this interesting..."
"You'll notice..."
"The pattern shows..."

ACTIVE VOICE REQUIRED
=====================
NOT: "Value is captured by international companies"
YES: "International companies capture the value"

PATTERN LANGUAGE
================
Repeat the same pattern 2-3 times to lock it in.

STORYTELLING
============
Include at least one person or company doing it right.
Use their story to illustrate the mechanism.

AVOIDING
========
- Long hedging language
- Academic tone
- Vague generalities
- Complex jargon without explanation

LENGTH
======
600-800 words. Same as your show script length.

VOICE MARKERS (YOUR PHRASES)
============================
"One object. One economy."
"What would it take..."
"The real question is..."
"Here is what I found..."
"But here is what makes this interesting..."
"You'll notice..."
"That pattern..."
"Both sides made rational decisions."
"Did not bury his talent."

KEY PRINCIPLE
=============
You are an investigator showing how systems work.
WHERE DOES THE MONEY ACTUALLY GO?
WHO IS POSITIONED TO CAPTURE IT?

End every briefing with: "This is The Zetu Atlas. One question. One economy. Together."
